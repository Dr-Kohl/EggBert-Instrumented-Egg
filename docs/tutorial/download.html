Download and save your recording

  1. Finish the experiment and wait for EggBert to show DONE or the completed recording result.
  2. Connect EggBert to your computer with a USB data cable.
  3. Open the Capture Viewer in Chrome or Edge. Click Connect EggBert, select EggBert’s USB serial port (it may appear as Pico - Board CDC) in the browser chooser, then click Connect. The COM port number may vary.
  4. Click Download to transfer the stored recording and display its graph. A verified transfer releases the USB connection automatically.
  5. Zoom in, click Measure Δt, then click two points on the chart to measure elapsed time. Drag cursor A or B to refine the interval. Use Focus event or Full capture to adjust the view.
  6. Click Save .egg to keep the original recording. Use Export CSV to save data for Excel.

Version 2: Automatic, Low-g and High-g

V2 recordings contain both accelerometers at each sample time. Automatic uses low-g for normal motion and switches the entire X/Y/Z vector to high-g when any low-g axis reaches 12 g. It returns when all axes on both sensors are below 8 g. Select Low-g or High-g to inspect either stream separately.

Both original streams remain in the .egg file. CSV includes both streams, the automatic trace and its per-sample source. High-g uses factory scaling and has independent offsets; small steps can appear at transitions. V1 and Beam retain their single low-g stream.

Save the .egg file before erasing the recording on EggBert.
