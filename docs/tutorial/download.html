Download and save your recording

  1. Finish the experiment and wait for EggBert to show DONE or the completed recording result.
  2. Connect EggBert to your computer with a USB data cable.
  3. Open the Capture Viewer in Chrome or Edge. Click Connect EggBert, select Pico - Board CDC in the browser chooser, then click Connect. The COM port number may vary.
  4. Click Download to transfer the stored recording and display its graph. A verified transfer releases the USB connection automatically.
  5. Zoom in and click two points on the chart to measure elapsed time. Drag cursor A or B to refine the interval. Use Focus event or Full capture to adjust the view.
  6. Click Save .egg to keep the original recording. Use Export CSV to save data for Excel.

Save the .egg file before erasing the recording on EggBert.
